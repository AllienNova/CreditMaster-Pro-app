### 14. Debt Strategy Analyzer

**Purpose**: Compare debt payoff methods and find optimal strategy
**Files**: `src/app/credit-builder/debt-strategy/page.tsx`, `error.tsx`, `loading.tsx`
**Lines of Code**: 1,050+

**Features**:
- **3 payoff methods**:

  *Avalanche Method*:
  - Pay highest interest rate first
  - Mathematically optimal
  - Saves most money
  - Pros: Fastest payoff, least interest
  - Cons: Slower initial wins
  - Best for: High-interest debt, math-minded users

  *Snowball Method*:
  - Pay smallest balance first
  - Psychological motivation
  - Quick early wins
  - Pros: Motivating victories, simplifies accounts
  - Cons: Costs more interest
  - Best for: Need motivation, multiple small debts

  *Hybrid Method*:
  - Balance of both approaches
  - Medium-sized high-interest debts first
  - Compromise strategy
  - Pros: Balance of savings & motivation
  - Cons: Not optimal for any single metric
  - Best for: Mixed debt portfolio

- **Real-time calculations**:
  - Total interest paid
  - Time to payoff (months/years)
  - Monthly payment allocation
  - Total amount paid
  - Payoff schedule (month-by-month)
  - Comparison charts

- **4 consolidation options**:

  *Personal Loan*:
  - APR: 10.5%
  - Term: 48 months
  - Impact: Neutral
  - Requirements: Credit 640+, stable income
  - Pros: Fixed payment, lower rate, single payment
  - Cons: Requires good credit, origination fees

  *Balance Transfer Card*:
  - APR: 0% (promo period)
  - Term: 18 months
  - Impact: Positive
  - Requirements: Credit 700+, low utilization
  - Pros: 0% for 12-21 months, saves interest
  - Cons: Transfer fee, excellent credit needed

  *HELOC (Home Equity)*:
  - APR: 7.5%
  - Term: 120 months
  - Impact: Neutral
  - Requirements: Home ownership, 20% equity
  - Pros: Lowest rates, tax deductible, flexible
  - Cons: Home is collateral, closing costs

  *Debt Management Plan*:
  - APR: 8%
  - Term: 48 months
  - Impact: Negative
  - Requirements: Unsecured debt, ability to pay
  - Pros: Reduced rates, single payment, guidance
  - Cons: Must close cards, shows on credit report

- **Debt portfolio tracker**:
  - Add/edit/remove debts
  - Balance tracking
  - Interest rate monitoring
  - Minimum payment calculation
  - Debt type categorization

- **Monthly budget optimizer**:
  - Budget slider
  - Extra payment calculation
  - Quick preset amounts
  - Impact visualization

**User Value**:
- Mathematical precision vs. manual guessing
- Saves $1,000s in interest with optimal method
- Reduces payoff time by months/years
- Provides motivation through progress tracking
- Professional-grade analysis for free
