## Executive Summary

This roadmap outlines prioritized enhancements for Fynvita based on competitive analysis of Range.com and 12+ financial platforms. The strategy focuses on Fynvita's unique position as the **only platform combining credit repair, budgeting, wealth management, and trading**.

### Strategic Priorities

1. **Solidify Credit Advantage** - No wealth competitor has credit tools
2. **Capture Post-Mint Users** - Free budgeting with upgrade path
3. **Differentiate with AI** - Multi-model coaching is unique
4. **Enable Real Trading** - Complete the investing story

**Feature Descriptions**:

- **Spending Pattern Analysis**: AI analyzes transaction history to identify recurring patterns, impulse purchases, and spending triggers (time of day, location, emotional states inferred from patterns).

- **Risk Tolerance Profiling**: Dynamic assessment combining questionnaire responses, actual investment behavior, and market reaction patterns to build accurate risk profiles.

- **Psychological Trigger Detection**: Identifies behavioral finance biases (loss aversion, anchoring, mental accounting) and provides real-time interventions.

- **Personalized Coaching Sessions**: AI-generated coaching content tailored to user's financial personality, goals, and current challenges.

- **Emotional Spending Alerts**: Proactive notifications when spending patterns suggest emotional or impulsive behavior (e.g., late-night shopping, stress spending).

**Key Deliverables**:

- [ ] Personalized coaching content generation system
