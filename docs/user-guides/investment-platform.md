## Getting Started

### Accessing the Investment Platform

1. Log in to your CreditMaster Pro account
2. Navigate to the **Investments** tab in the main menu
3. Your portfolio dashboard will load automatically

### First-Time Setup

If you're new to the investment platform:

1. Click **"Add Your First Holding"** on the empty portfolio screen
2. Enter your stock symbol (e.g., AAPL for Apple)
3. Enter the quantity and purchase price
4. Click **"Add Holding"** to save

## Portfolio Dashboard

### Overview

The Portfolio Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of your investment portfolio:

- **Total Value**: Current market value of all holdings
- **Total Gain/Loss**: Overall profit or loss since purchase
- **Today's Change**: Daily performance
- **Asset Allocation**: Breakdown by asset type (stocks, ETFs, crypto, etc.)
- **Performance Chart**: Historical performance over time

### Time Period Selection

View your portfolio performance over different time periods:

- **1D**: Last 24 hours
- **1W**: Last week
- **1M**: Last month (default)
- **3M**: Last 3 months
- **6M**: Last 6 months
- **1Y**: Last year
- **ALL**: Since inception

Click any period button to update the chart and metrics.

### Refreshing Data

- Click the **Refresh** button to update all prices and metrics
- Data automatically refreshes every 30 seconds
- Real-time updates are available via WebSocket connection

## Managing Holdings

### Adding a New Holding

1. Navigate to **Investments > Holdings**
2. Click **"Add Holding"** button
3. Fill in the required information:
   - **Symbol**: Stock ticker (e.g., TSLA)
   - **Quantity**: Number of shares
   - **Purchase Price**: Price per share when purchased
   - **Purchase Date**: Date of purchase
   - **Asset Type**: Stock, ETF, Crypto, etc.
4. Click **"Save"** to add the holding

### Editing a Holding

1. Find the holding in your holdings list
2. Click the **Edit** icon (pencil)
3. Update the information
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Deleting a Holding

1. Find the holding in your holdings list
2. Click the **Delete** icon (trash can)
3. Confirm the deletion
4. The holding will be removed from your portfolio

### Searching and Filtering

- **Search**: Use the search bar to find holdings by symbol or name
- **Filter by Type**: Select asset type (All, Stocks, ETFs, Crypto, Bonds)
- **Sort**: Sort by value, gain %, or symbol

## Stock Analysis

### Analyzing a Stock

1. Navigate to **Investments > Analyze**
2. Enter a stock symbol (e.g., AAPL)
3. Click **"Analyze"** to generate AI-powered analysis

### Understanding the Analysis

The stock analysis includes four main sections:

#### 1. Technical Analysis

- **RSI (Relative Strength Index)**: Momentum indicator (0-100)
  - Below 30: Oversold (potential buy)
  - Above 70: Overbought (potential sell)
- **MACD**: Trend-following momentum indicator
- **Moving Averages**: Price trend indicators

#### 2. Fundamental Analysis

- **P/E Ratio**: Price-to-earnings ratio
- **EPS**: Earnings per share
- **Market Cap**: Total market value
- **Dividend Yield**: Annual dividend as % of price
- **Revenue**: Company's total revenue

#### 3. Sentiment Analysis

- **Sentiment Score**: Overall market sentiment (-1 to +1)
- **News Sources**: Number of analyzed news articles
- **Social Media**: Sentiment from social platforms

#### 4. AI Recommendation

- **Recommendation**: Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, Sell, or Strong Sell
- **Confidence Score**: AI's confidence in the recommendation (0-100%)
- **Target Price**: Predicted price target
- **Summary**: AI-generated analysis summary
- **Bullish Factors**: Positive indicators
- **Bearish Factors**: Negative indicators

## AI-Powered Recommendations

### How It Works

Our AI Stock Analyst uses:

- **Machine Learning Models**: Trained on historical market data
- **Real-Time Data**: Current prices, volume, and market conditions
- **News Sentiment**: Analysis of recent news and social media
- **Technical Indicators**: RSI, MACD, moving averages, and more
- **Fundamental Metrics**: Financial statements and ratios

### Interpreting Recommendations

- **Strong Buy**: High confidence in significant upside potential
- **Buy**: Positive outlook with moderate upside
- **Hold**: Neutral outlook, maintain current position
- **Sell**: Negative outlook, consider reducing position
- **Strong Sell**: High confidence in significant downside risk

**Important**: AI recommendations are for informational purposes only. Always do your own research and consult with a financial advisor before making investment decisions.

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, all data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security practices.

**Q: How often are prices updated?**
A: Prices update every 30 seconds during market hours. Real-time updates are available via WebSocket.

**Q: Can I import my existing portfolio?**
A: Yes, you can manually add all your holdings or contact support for bulk import assistance.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What markets are supported?**
A: We support US stocks, ETFs, and major cryptocurrencies. International markets coming soon.

**Q: How accurate are the AI recommendations?**
A: Our AI has a historical accuracy rate of 75-80%, but past performance doesn't guarantee future results.

**Q: Can I export my portfolio data?**
A: Yes, you can export to CSV or PDF from the portfolio dashboard.

### Troubleshooting

**Q: Why isn't my portfolio updating?**
A: Try refreshing the page or checking your internet connection. If the issue persists, contact support.

**Q: Why is a stock showing incorrect data?**
A: Market data may be delayed by 15 minutes for free accounts. Premium accounts get real-time data.

**Q: How do I delete my portfolio?**
A: Contact support to permanently delete your portfolio data.
