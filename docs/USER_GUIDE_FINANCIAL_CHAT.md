# Financial Chat User Guide

**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Last Updated**: January 5, 2026  
**Platform**: Web & Mobile

---

## 📱 Overview

The Financial Chat feature provides an AI-powered conversational interface to help you manage your finances, get investment advice, plan budgets, and optimize your financial strategy. Available on both web and mobile platforms.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Accessing Financial Chat

**Web**:

1. Log in to CreditMaster Pro
2. Navigate to **Financial Intelligence** → **Chat**
3. Click **"New Chat"** to start a conversation

**Mobile**:

1. Open the CreditMaster Pro app
2. Tap the **"Financial Intelligence"** tab
3. Select **"Chat"**
4. Tap the **"+"** button to create a new chat

---

## 💬 Using the Chat Interface

### Starting a New Chat Session

1. **Create a Session**:
   - Click/Tap **"New Chat"** button
   - Optionally, give your chat a descriptive title (e.g., "Retirement Planning", "Debt Payoff Strategy")
   - If no title is provided, it defaults to "New Chat"

2. **Send Your First Message**:
   - Type your question in the input field
   - Click **"Send"** or press **Enter** (web) / **Send** button (mobile)
   - The AI will analyze your question and provide a personalized response

### Example Questions

**Portfolio & Investments**:

- "How should I allocate my investment portfolio?"
- "What's the best strategy for my 401(k)?"
- "Should I invest in stocks or bonds right now?"
- "Analyze my current portfolio performance"

**Budget & Savings**:

- "Help me create a monthly budget"
- "How can I save more money each month?"
- "What's the best way to build an emergency fund?"
- "Review my spending patterns"

**Debt Management**:

- "What's the best way to pay off my credit card debt?"
- "Should I use the debt avalanche or snowball method?"
- "How can I consolidate my debts?"
- "Calculate my debt-to-income ratio"

**Tax Planning**:

- "How can I reduce my tax liability?"
- "What tax deductions am I eligible for?"
- "Should I contribute to a Roth or Traditional IRA?"

**Retirement Planning**:

- "Am I on track for retirement?"
- "How much should I save for retirement?"
- "When can I afford to retire?"

**General Financial Questions**:

- "What's my net worth?"
- "How's my financial health?"
- "Give me a financial overview"

## 🎯 Understanding AI Responses

### Response Components

1. **Direct Answer**: The AI provides a clear, actionable answer to your question

2. **Personalized Insights**: Based on your:
   - Current portfolio holdings
   - Financial goals
   - Risk tolerance
   - Income and expenses
   - Debt levels

3. **Action Suggestions**: Specific steps you can take, such as:
   - "Review your current debt balances"
   - "Consider increasing your 401(k) contribution"
   - "Set up automatic savings transfers"

4. **Data Visualizations** (when applicable):
   - Portfolio allocation charts
   - Spending breakdowns
   - Debt payoff timelines

### Intent Detection

The AI automatically detects what you're asking about:

- **Portfolio Advice**: Investment allocation questions
- **Budget Help**: Budget planning and tracking
- **Debt Strategy**: Debt payoff strategies
- **Savings Goals**: Savings planning
- **Investment Analysis**: Stock/fund analysis
- **Tax Planning**: Tax optimization
- **Retirement Planning**: Retirement preparation
- **Risk Assessment**: Risk tolerance evaluation

## 📋 Managing Chat Sessions

### Viewing Chat History

**Web**:

- All your chat sessions appear in the left sidebar
- Click any session to view its conversation history
- Sessions are sorted by most recent activity

**Mobile**:

- Swipe from the left edge or tap the menu icon
- Your chat sessions appear in a list
- Tap any session to open it

### Renaming a Chat Session

**Web**:

1. Hover over the chat session in the sidebar
2. Click the **"..."** menu icon
3. Select **"Rename"**
4. Enter a new title and press **Enter**

**Mobile**:

1. Long-press on a chat session
2. Select **"Rename"** from the menu
3. Enter a new title and tap **"Save"**

### Deleting a Chat Session

**Web**:

1. Hover over the chat session in the sidebar
2. Click the **"..."** menu icon
3. Select **"Delete"**
4. Confirm the deletion

**Mobile**:

1. Swipe left on a chat session
2. Tap the **"Delete"** button
3. Confirm the deletion

**Note**: Deleted chats are archived, not permanently removed. Contact support if you need to recover a deleted chat.

## 🔍 Quick Actions & Suggestions

### Suggestion Chips (Mobile)

On mobile, you'll see quick action chips below the input field:

- **"View Portfolio"**: Get a portfolio overview
- **"Budget Help"**: Start budget planning
- **"Debt Strategy"**: Get debt payoff advice
- **"Savings Goals"**: Plan your savings

Tap any chip to quickly send that query.

### Context-Aware Responses

The AI remembers your conversation context:

- You can ask follow-up questions without repeating information
- The AI references previous messages in the conversation
- Context is maintained throughout the entire chat session

**Example**:

```
You: "How should I allocate my portfolio?"
AI: "Based on your moderate risk tolerance, I recommend 60% stocks, 30% bonds, 10% cash..."

You: "What about international stocks?"
AI: "For your portfolio, I'd suggest allocating 20% of your stock portion to international markets..."
```

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

### Data Protection

- **Zero Trust Security**: Every request is authenticated and validated
- **Row Level Security**: You can only access your own chat sessions
- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **No Data Sharing**: Your conversations are private and never shared

### Input Sanitization

- All messages are sanitized to prevent security vulnerabilities
- Maximum message length: 2000 characters
- HTML and scripts are automatically stripped

### Session Security

- Sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity
- You're automatically logged out after 24 hours
- Re-authentication required for sensitive operations

## ⚡ Performance & Optimization

### Response Times

- **Typical Response**: 2-5 seconds
- **Complex Queries**: 5-10 seconds (e.g., portfolio analysis)
- **Simple Questions**: 1-2 seconds (e.g., greetings)

### Offline Support (Mobile)

- **Read-Only Mode**: View previous conversations offline
- **Sync on Reconnect**: New messages sync when connection is restored
- **Offline Indicator**: Clear indication when offline

### Caching

- Recent conversations are cached for instant loading
- Messages are cached for 1 minute
- Sessions are cached for 2 minutes

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**"Failed to send message"**:

- Check your internet connection
- Verify you're logged in
- Try refreshing the page/app
- Check if you've exceeded rate limits (60 messages/minute)

**"Session not found"**:

- The session may have been deleted
- Try creating a new session
- Contact support if the issue persists

**Slow responses**:

- Complex queries take longer to process
- Check your internet connection speed
- Try during off-peak hours

**Messages not appearing**:

- Refresh the page/app
- Check if the session is archived
- Clear your browser cache (web)

## 📊 Best Practices

### Getting the Best Responses

1. **Be Specific**: "How should I allocate $10,000 in my Roth IRA?" vs. "Investment advice?"
2. **Provide Context**: Mention your goals, timeline, and risk tolerance
3. **Ask Follow-ups**: Dig deeper into topics that interest you
4. **Use Natural Language**: Talk to the AI like you would a financial advisor

### Organizing Your Chats

1. **Use Descriptive Titles**: "2026 Tax Planning" vs. "New Chat"
2. **One Topic Per Chat**: Keep retirement planning separate from debt strategy
3. **Archive Old Chats**: Delete chats you no longer need
4. **Review Regularly**: Revisit old chats to track your progress
